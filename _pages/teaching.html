Tutor of the course Fundamentals of Mathematics II (Linear Algebra and Vector Analysis)

Degree course in Chemical Sciences, Sapienza University of Rome
March 2023 – July 2023

One semester of weekly 120-minute tutorial lectures. Activities included reviewing the core concepts covered during the main lectures, presenting worked examples, and providing step-by-step guidance for solving examination-style problems.

Topics:

  • Fundamentals of linear algebra
  • Scalar functions of several real variables
  • Vector-valued functions of several real variables
  • Differential calculus for functions of several real variables
  • Integral calculus for functions of several real variables
