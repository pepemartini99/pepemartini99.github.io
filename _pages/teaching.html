Topics: Fundamentals of linear algebra, scalar functions of multiple real variables, vector-valued functions of multiple real variables, differential and integral calculus for functions of multiple real variables.
### Tutor of the courses "Geometry" and "Mathematical Analysis", for the degree course in Civil and Industrial Engineering, at Sapienza University *09/2022 – 02/2023* One semester of 5-hours sessions of interactive tutoring with small groups of students: review of the core concepts covered during the main lectures, with examples and step-by-step guide to the resolutions of problems. Topics: Mathematical analysis of real scalar functions of one real variable; basic linear algebra and Cartesian geometry; fundamentals of the theory of conic sections.
### Tutor of the Course of "Physics I (Classical Mechanics with Laboratory)", for the degree course in Chemical Sciences, at Sapienza University *03/2022 – 09/2022* One semester of one 120 minutes long tutorial lecture per week: review of the core concepts covered during the main lectures, with examples and step-by-step guide to the resolutions of exam-type problems. Topics: Introduction to the experimental method of physical sciences, fundamentals of statistics and probability for the experimental sciences; kinematics of an ideal point-particle, Newtonian mechanics of point-particles and rigi bodies, conserved quantities in mechanical systems, classical wave mechanics.
