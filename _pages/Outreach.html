Outreach Activity: Fundamentally Perfect - Discovering the Nature of the Platonic Solids

(An activity devised in collaboration with Vincenzo Rossi and presented for the first time at the European Researchers' Night – SHARPER 2026)

The aim of this activity is to guide students in discovering the Platonic solids (also known as regular polyhedra), starting from intuition, proceeding through hands-on constructions, and culminating in the identification of the fundamental abstract properties that characterize these objects. By experimenting with magnetic building blocks and constructing cardboard models, students explore and identify what it means for a solid to be regular, how regular three-dimensional objects can be built, and which constraints regularity imposes on their construction. The fundamental goal is to help young students understand that mathematics does not consist of juggling arcane symbols and obscure formulas. Rather, it is the fundamental language we use to understand the physical Universe, and it is a fundamental part of human culture, underling not only the foundations of modern science but also the ways in which we define the concepts of beauty and perfection.
