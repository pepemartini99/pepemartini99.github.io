About me
======

I am Pierfrancesco Martini, a PhD student in mathematics at [Gran Sasso Science Institute](https://www.gssi.it/), under the supervision of [Prof. Gabriele Benomio](https://sites.google.com/view/benomio). [Here](https://www.gssi.it/people/students/students-maths/item/24624-martini-pierfrancesco) you can find my institutional page.
In my research, I use tools from partial differential equations to investigate problems in General Relativity.
My background is in theoretical and mathematical physics: I graduated with honors in theoretical physics from [Sapienza, University of Rome](https://www.phys.uniroma1.it/en). My Master thesis, conducted under the supervision of [Prof. Gianluca Panati](https://research.uniroma1.it/researcher/c45d41dd8bebc608e1ec89bcf8bcc79c72cc71e75477d45f79c3128f) and [Prof. Domenico Monaco](https://sites.google.com/view/dmonaco), deals with the topological structures underlying transport phenomena within flat-band crystals.



Academic interests
------
My main research interest are problems from physics which can be approached combining techniques from different fields of mathematics, like the theory of differential equations, differential geometry and topology. My current research project focuses on the stability of higher dimensional black hole spacetimes, and in how studying linear systems of hyperbolic PDEs can be helpful to understand this problem.
