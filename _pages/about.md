About me
======

I am a PhD student in mathematics at GSSI, under the supervision of Gabriele Benomio. In my research, I use tools from partial differential equations to investigate problems in General Relativity.
My background is in theoretical and mathematical physics: I graduated with honors in theoretical physics from Sapienza, University of Rome. My Master thesis, conducted under the supervision of Professor Gianluca Panati and Professor Domenico Monaco, deals with the topological structures underlying transport phenomena within flat-band crystals.
