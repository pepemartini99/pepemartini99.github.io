About me
======

I am a PhD student in mathematics at [Gran Sasso Science Institute](https://www.gssi.it/), under the supervision of [Prof. Gabriele Benomio](https://sites.google.com/view/benomio). In my research, I use tools from partial differential equations to investigate problems in General Relativity.
My background is in theoretical and mathematical physics: I graduated with honors in theoretical physics from [Sapienza, University of Rome](https://www.phys.uniroma1.it/en). My Master thesis, conducted under the supervision of [Prof. Gianluca Panati](https://research.uniroma1.it/researcher/c45d41dd8bebc608e1ec89bcf8bcc79c72cc71e75477d45f79c3128f) and [Prof. Domenico Monaco](https://sites.google.com/view/dmonaco), deals with the topological structures underlying transport phenomena within flat-band crystals.



Academic interests
------
I am interested in problems from physics which can be approached combining techniques from different fields of mathematics, like differential geometry, topology and the theory of differential equations. 



Personal interests
------
In my private time I enjoy learning about the history of mathematical sciences (expecially from the Hellenistic era), reading fantasy books, and practicing martial arts and combat sports.
