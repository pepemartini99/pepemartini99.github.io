About me
======

I am Pierfrancesco Martini, a PhD student in mathematics at [Gran Sasso Science Institute](https://www.gssi.it/), under the supervision of [Prof. Gabriele Benomio](https://sites.google.com/view/benomio).
In my research, I use tools from partial differential equations to investigate problems in General Relativity.
Here you can find my [CV](/files/CV.pdf) and [institutional page](https://www.gssi.it/people/students/students-maths/item/24624-martini-pierfrancesco).




Academic interests
------
My research revolves around the mathematical analysis of nonlinear partial differential equations, in particular the ones arising from physics. My primary interest lies in the analysis of Einstein's equations of general relativity. My current research project focuses on the stability of higher dimensional black hole spacetimes, and in how studying linear systems of hyperbolic PDEs can be helpful to understand this problem.
